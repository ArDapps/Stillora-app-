FAQ

Questions, answered

Everything you need to know about turning images, video, and audio into share-ready MP4s.

Is Stillora free to use?
Yes — Stillora is 100% free, with no plans, no paywalls, and no credit card required. Every export is free and there is no watermark on any video.
Can I combine images, video, and audio in one video?
Yes. Stillora mixes still images, video clips, and a backing audio track into a single MP4 export. Images can be JPEG, PNG or WebP; video can be MP4, MOV or AVI; audio can be MP3, WAV, M4A, AAC or OGG.
Which social media formats does Stillora support?
Stillora includes built-in presets for Reels, TikTok and Stories (1080×1920, 9:16), YouTube Shorts (1080×1920), YouTube long video (1920×1080, 16:9), Square posts (1080×1080, 1:1), and Original image size.
Do I need an account to use Stillora?
No. You can start creating in your browser instantly — no sign-up and no account required.
What format does Stillora export?
Stillora exports a share-ready H.264 MP4 with AAC audio at 30 FPS, rendered server-side with native FFmpeg.
What platforms is Stillora available on?
Stillora is available now on every platform — a web app in any browser, a desktop app for Mac, Windows and Linux, and mobile apps for iOS and Android (including tablets).
