## Store Listing

Short description:
Create polished image-to-video posts with local editing tools.

Full description:
Stillora helps creators turn images, clips, audio, and ideas into polished short videos for social platforms.

Build videos with platform-ready presets, local media tools, voice narration, audio controls, export previews, and a private on-device gallery. Stillora is designed for fast creative workflows without sending your personal media to a remote editing service.

What you can do:
- Import images, videos, and audio from your device.
- Choose social video presets for common formats.
- Add voice narration and background audio.
- Preview before export.
- Save and share finished MP4 videos.
- Keep exported projects in a local gallery.

Privacy-first editing:
Stillora processes your creative media on your device. Your selected files and recordings are used to create your videos and are not uploaded by the editor workflow.

Great for:
- Social posts
- Product visuals
- Reels and shorts
- Story videos
- Simple promotional videos
- Personal creative edits
