# 01 · Product Overview

## What Stillora is

Stillora is a **free, no-timeline video maker** that turns still images, video clips, and audio tracks into one polished, platform-ready MP4. Instead of a complex editor with keyframes and tracks, it's built around simple, guided flows — pick media, choose a format, export.

**Category:** MP4 video maker / "still-to-video" creator tool for social media.

## The core promise

- **Free** — no paywall, no plans, no credit card. (See accuracy flags re: sign-in for export on web.)
- **No watermark** on any export.
- **On-device** — the local engine processes video on the user's own device; files don't depend on cloud storage. Strong privacy story: *"Files stay on this device."*
- **Social-first** — every tool exports in the exact dimensions each platform wants (Reels, TikTok, Shorts, YouTube, square).
- **Everywhere** — the same product on web, phone, and desktop.

## Who it's for

- Content creators and social media managers who need quick, correctly-sized clips.
- Podcasters, musicians, and reviewers turning audio + a still into a shareable video.
- Anyone who wants to make a video from photos without learning an editor.

## The three-step story (already used on the site)

1. **Drop your media** — photos, clips, or both.
2. **Choose your format** — Reels, TikTok, YouTube, square, and more.
3. **Export your MP4** — share-ready in seconds, no watermark.

## How it makes money

Stillora is monetized with **Loopara sponsored ad banners** shown inside the app (in-house ad network) — **not** subscriptions. There is no active Pro tier or paywall in the shipping product. (A RevenueCat "Pro" model exists only in setup docs, not in the live code — see accuracy flags.)
