## The 30-second summary

Stillora is a **free MP4 video maker** available on **web, iOS, macOS, Android, and Windows** (Linux too). It has one flagship tool — **Create** — that turns any mix of photos, video clips, and audio into a single share-ready video sized for Reels, TikTok, Shorts, YouTube, or square posts. Around it sit several focused tools: **HTML → Video**, **Loop Images** (batch), **Watermark**, **Remove Silence**, **Speed**, **Convert** (image format converter), and a local **Library**.

Everything the local engine does runs **on the user's device** — a strong privacy angle. Exports carry **no watermark** and go up to **4K** on mobile / **1080p** on web.
