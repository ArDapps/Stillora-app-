# 02 · Complete Feature List

Every user-facing feature, in plain language. Grouped by tool. Platform availability is summarized here and detailed in [03-Platform-Matrix.md](03-Platform-Matrix.md).

---

## 1. Create — the flagship tool ⭐
**Turn photos and clips into one share-ready MP4, in three steps: Upload → Audio → Export.**

- Combine multiple photos and/or video clips into one video.
- Reorder clips by dragging, trim each clip's duration, remove individual clips.
- Slideshow mode for multiple images, with animated transitions; also handles single videos and mixed photo+video timelines.
- Per-clip duration with quick-picks (1s → 30m) or type any value.
- Per-clip volume control for video (mute/unmute + 0–100%).
- Add a music/audio soundtrack **or** record your own voice narration.
- "Fit to audio" — auto-match the video length to your soundtrack.
- **Aspect-ratio presets:** 9:16 Reels/Shorts/TikTok, 1:1 Square, 4:5 Portrait, 16:9 YouTube, or Original.
- **Fit or Fill** framing (show the whole image, or crop to fill the frame).
- **Quality tiers (mobile):** 720p / 1080p / 2K / 4K with a live estimated file size. (Web exports at 1080p.)
- **Visual effects (preview):** Glow, Pan & Zoom (Ken Burns), Float, Shake.
- **Transitions:** Fade, Swipe, Zoom, Slide Up/Down, Glitch, Flash, Pulse. (Fade is baked into export; others are preview-focused.)
- **One-tap Styles** that bundle effect + transition: Clean, Neon, Cinematic, Dreamy, Energetic.
- Live preview matching the final frame; exports MP4 with **no watermark**.

**Availability:** All platforms. · **Price:** Free.

---

## 2. Voice Narration
**Record a voiceover directly in the app and add it to your video.**

- Record, re-record, or remove narration with a live waveform display and volume control.
- Reached from the "Add Soundtrack" step inside Create.
- Privacy copy: *"Your audio is secured and used only for this conversion."*

**Availability:** All platforms (uses device mic). · **Price:** Free.

---

## 3. HTML → Video  *(marketed as NEW)*
**Turn an animated HTML page, snippet, or web URL into a clean MP4.**

- **Three input modes:** paste HTML, upload an `.html`/`.htm` file, or enter a URL.
- Frame-accurate capture — CSS animations, timers, and scripts step in lockstep for smooth output.
- **Output sizes:** 9:16 Vertical, 1:1 Square, 16:9 Landscape, 4:5 Portrait.
- **Frame rate:** 24 / 30 / 60 fps.
- **Duration:** up to 60 seconds.
- Optional audio track (voice-over or music) muxed onto the render.
- Live preview, save to device / camera roll, and share.

**Availability:** All platforms (web + mobile). · **Price:** Free — the most openly accessible tool.

---

## 4. Loop Images — batch renderer
**Add many images and render a separate MP4 for each one (never merged).**

- Add many images at once (JPG, PNG, WebP); on web also **PDF** (first page becomes the image).
- Pick **one** output size + **one** duration + **one** Fit/Fill mode applied to all.
- **Quality tiers:** 720p / 1080p / 2K / 4K with per-video size estimate (mobile).
- Per-image status badges (Ready / Rendering / Done / Failed); share or download each result.
- Web: "Download all" grabs every finished MP4 at once (up to 30 files).

**Availability:** All platforms. · **Price:** Free.

---

## 5. Watermark / Overlay
**Stack logos, images, or videos on top of a base video.**

- Add one or more overlays; drag, resize, and give each a time window (appears only during part of the clip).
- Overlays can be images or videos; the base video keeps its own audio.
- Live looping preview; exports MP4 at source resolution.

**Availability:** ⚠️ Desktop only (macOS / Windows / Linux) for now — in-app copy says *"iPhone & Android export is coming next."* Hidden on mobile. · **Price:** Free.

---

## 6. Remove Silence
**Upload a talking video and auto-cut the silent gaps.**

- Sensitivity slider: Gentle → Balanced → Aggressive.
- Optionally speed the result 1x–4x.
- Audio options: keep, remove, or replace with a new soundtrack (video loops to match).
- **Quality tiers:** 720p / 1080p / 2K / 4K with output size + estimate.

**Availability:** ⚠️ Desktop only (macOS / Windows / Linux) for now; hidden on mobile. · **Price:** Free.

---

## 7. Speed
**Upload a video and speed it up.**

- Speeds: 1x, 2x, 3x, 4x (shows resulting length).
- Audio: keep, mute, or add a new soundtrack (sped video loops to match).
- **Quality tiers:** 720p / 1080p / 2K / 4K.

**Availability:** ⚠️ Desktop **and iPhone (iOS)**; hidden on Android for now. · **Price:** Free.

---

## 8. Convert — image format converter
**Batch-convert images into JPEG or PNG.**

- Input: HEIC, WebP, TIFF, BMP, and more. Output: JPEG or PNG.
- Batch-select many images; add/remove/clear.
- Saves to Photos (mobile) or a chosen folder / "Downloads › Stillora Converted" (desktop).
- Reports how many converted / failed.

**Availability:** All platforms. · **Price:** Free.

---

## 9. Library — local gallery
**Every exported video, stored on-device.**

- Grid (wide screens) and list (phone) views with thumbnail, preset, resolution, duration, date.
- Tap to play; multi-select to delete (with Select All).
- Copy: *"Exports are stored on this phone. Nothing here depends on cloud storage."*

**Availability:** All platforms. · **Price:** Free.

---

## 10. Batch Converter *(web/desktop naming for Loop Images)*
On web the batch tool also accepts **PDFs** (rasterizes the first page) and offers a **"Download all"** button — up to 30 files at once.

## 11. Info / Profile & Settings
- Brand panel, Privacy Policy and Terms links, settings, and a "rate the app" prompt.

**Availability:** All platforms.
